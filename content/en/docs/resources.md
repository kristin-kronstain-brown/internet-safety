These are the outside resources this guide draws on, grouped by what you need them for.

## If something has already happened

- [Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/) removes nude or sexually explicit images of someone who was under 18 when the image was made. Free, and the image never leaves the device.
- [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/) takes reports of child sexual exploitation. The 24-hour call center is 1-800-843-5678.
- [National Center for Missing and Exploited Children](https://www.missingkids.org/)

## For parents

- [NetSmartz](https://www.ncmec.org/netsmartz/home)
- [Family Online Safety Institute](https://fosi.org/parenting/)
- [Common Sense Media Parent Guides](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/parents-ultimate-guides)
- [Connect Safely Parent Guides](https://connectsafely.org/parentguides/)
- [Department of Homeland Security Know2Protect](https://www.dhs.gov/know2protect/all-resources)
- UK National Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children (NSPCC)
    - [Age-based advice](https://www.nspcc.org.uk/keeping-children-safe/online-safety/access-to-technology-for-different-ages/)
    - [Grooming](https://www.nspcc.org.uk/keeping-children-safe/types-of-abuse/grooming/)
    - [Teaching kids about AI](https://www.nspcc.org.uk/keeping-children-safe/online-safety/talking-to-children-about-ai/)

## Local programs

These two are specific to their counties. They are worth a look as examples of what a local program can offer, and worth checking whether your own county runs something similar.

- [Genesee County, MI Police Department GHOST certification (Genesee Human Oppression Strike Team)](https://www.gcsomichigan.com/ghost)
- [Guilford County, NC required safety videos](https://www.gcsnc.com/families/safety-emergency-management/protecting-our-students-required-safety-videos)
